About IKE K VENTURES

We are an in-person over-the-counter (OTC) medicine seller serving residents, market traders, and families around Apremdo Market and the 2BN Garrison area in Takoradi.

Our counter provides everyday non-prescription medicines and basic health supplies so you can quickly pick up what you need close to home. We do not sell medicines online or offer medical diagnosis.

What We Carry

Everyday over-the-counter categories available at our counter. Call ahead if you want to check a specific item.

  • Pain, Headache & Fever

    Everyday painkillers, paracetamol, body pain relief, and fever syrups for adults and children.

  • Cough, Cold & Flu

    Cough mixtures, throat lozenges, nasal relief, and common cold or allergy remedies.

  • Stomach & Rehydration (ORS)

    Oral Rehydration Salts (ORS), antacids, routine dewormers, and basic digestive relief.

  • First Aid & Wound Care

    Plasters, bandages, cotton wool, methylated spirit, antiseptic liquid, and ointments.

  • Vitamins & Blood Tonics

    Daily multivitamins, blood tonics, Vitamin C, folic acid, and routine supplements.

  • Malaria & Hygiene Essentials

    Approved over-the-counter malaria essentials, mosquito repellents, and basic personal care.

Note: We supply over-the-counter items only in person. Always read the medicine leaflet and visit a hospital or clinic if symptoms persist.

How to Find the Shop

We are not right on the main highway corner. From the 2BN Garrison Main Road, branch onto Apremdo Road and walk a short distance down until you see Apremdo Market on your left. Our shop is directly opposite the market on your right.
