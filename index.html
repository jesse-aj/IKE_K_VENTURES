About IKE K VENTURES

We are an in-person over-the-counter (OTC) medicine seller serving residents, market traders, and families around Apremdo Market and the 2BN Garrison area in Takoradi.

Our counter stocks everyday non-prescription medicines and basic first-aid supplies so you can quickly pick up what you need close to home. We do not sell medicines online or offer medical diagnosis.

What the Shop Offers

As an OTC medicine seller, we stock general non-prescription categories for everyday needs. Call ahead if you would like to confirm availability at the counter.

  • Pain, Headache & Fever

    Everyday painkillers, paracetamol, body pain relief, and fever syrups for adults and children.

  • Cough, Cold & Flu

    Cough mixtures, throat lozenges, nasal relief, and common cold or allergy remedies.

  • Stomach & Rehydration (ORS)

    Oral Rehydration Salts (ORS), antacids, routine dewormers, and basic digestive relief.

  • First Aid & Wound Care

    Plasters, bandages, cotton wool, methylated spirit, antiseptic liquid, and ointments.

  • Vitamins & Blood Tonics

    Daily multivitamins, blood tonics, Vitamin C, folic acid, and routine supplements.

  • Malaria & Hygiene Essentials

    Over-the-counter malaria care essentials, mosquito repellents, and basic personal care.

Important: IKE K VENTURES supplies over-the-counter items in person only. Always read the medicine leaflet carefully and visit a hospital or clinic if symptoms persist.
